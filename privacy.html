J and A's Clips — Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

J and A's Clips is a personal, single-user application built and run by an individual developer on their own computer. It is not a public service and does not have other users besides its developer.

What data the app handles

  • Video recordings the developer makes of their own gameplay, processed locally.
  • An access token for the developer's own TikTok (and other platform) account, obtained through that platform's official login flow, used only to post the developer's own clips to the developer's own account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from, or about, anyone other than its developer.
  • It does not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties.
  • It does not run on any server; everything is stored locally on the developer's own machine.

Third-party platforms

When the app posts a clip to TikTok (or another connected platform), that platform's own privacy policy governs how it handles the uploaded content.

Revoking access

The developer can disconnect the app from their TikTok account at any time from TikTok's own account settings, or by removing the stored access token from the app.

Contact
